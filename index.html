The Amazing Toyota gr86

Learn about this car.

The Toyota GR86 is widely considered one of the best entry-level sports cars on the market today. Developed in a joint partnership with Subaru (which sells the nearly identical Subaru BRZ), it is designed to be affordable, lightweight, and incredibly fun to drive. The Toyota GR86 represents something increasingly rare in the modern automotive landscape: a lightweight, naturally aspirated, rear-wheel-drive sports car built purely for the joy of driving. In an era where manufacturers are constantly chasing horsepower figures into the stratospheric 500-plus range and relying on heavy battery packs or complex turbocharged systems, the GR86 goes in the exact opposite direction. It serves as the spiritual successor to the legendary AE86 Corolla of the 1980s—a car celebrated for its drifting capabilities and balanced chassis—and it heavily improves upon the success of the first-generation GT86. The "GR" stands for Gazoo Racing, Toyota's motorsport division, which took an active role in tuning this second generation to ensure it delivers thrills on both winding canyon roads and closed track circuits. What makes the GR86 so special isn't merely its straight-line speed, although it is certainly quick enough to push you back in your seat. Instead, its magic lies in how it communicates with the driver. The steering is sharp and responsive, the seating position is low, and the suspension is tuned to provide a strong connection to the road without riding too stiffly. It's a car that rewards momentum and driver skill rather than just brute force. By keeping the curb weight down to roughly 2,850 pounds and ensuring optimal weight distribution through its front-engine, rear-wheel-drive layout, the GR86 offers nimble handling and a confidence-inspiring balance. Whether you are a novice learning the ropes of rear-wheel dynamics or a seasoned track veteran looking for a pure, analog driving experience, the GR86 stands as a testament to the fact that you don't need a massive budget or endless horsepower to have an incredibly rewarding time behind the wheel.
